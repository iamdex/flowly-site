Focus.
Then really rest.

A calm, private timer for working in blocks: real breaks, soundscapes that work offline and, if you want, the apps that distract you paused until the focus ends.

Download on the App Store Coming soon to the App Store for iPhone.

What it does

A few things, done with care. The timer, breaks and history are free, forever.

Free

Pomodoro or your own

25/5 with a long break every 4 focuses, or your own durations. The next phase starts only when you decide.

Free

Always in sight

The time left stays on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island. Widgets for Home and lock screen.

Free

Offline soundscapes

Rain, forest, sea, brown noise: day and night illustrations and sounds that need no network.

Plus

App blocking

Choose the apps that distract you: they’re paused only during focus and unlock by themselves, even if Flowly is closed.

Plus

Mix scenes

Add a quieter layer under the main scene. Rain and fireplace, café and brown noise.

Plus

Keep the screen on

During a session the screen stays on, so the time is always a glance away.

Flowly Plus

Two ways to unlock the same features. Prices in your currency are on the App Store.

Yearly

with a 7-day free trial

Renews every year; cancel any time in your iPhone settings.

Lifetime

one-time

A single purchase, no renewals.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics. Sessions, intentions and settings stay on your iPhone. The apps you choose to block are handled by iOS: Flowly can’t see which ones they are.
