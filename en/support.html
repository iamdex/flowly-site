Support

Frequently asked questions. Can’t find your answer? Write to me: I reply personally.

New iPhone: how do I get Plus back?

Open Flowly › Settings › Restore purchases, with the same Apple ID you bought it with.

How do I cancel the subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions › Flowly. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal.

I don’t see the time on the lock screen.

Check that Live Activities are on in Settings › Flowly Focus. The time appears while a phase is running.

App blocking doesn’t turn on.

It needs Plus and Screen Time permission. In Flowly › Settings › Deep focus, choose the apps and turn blocking on: the line under the switch shows the state. Apps are blocked only while a focus is running.

Do notifications from blocked apps arrive?

While an app is blocked its notifications may not arrive: iOS decides that. To silence everything, use a Focus mode and add Flowly Focus to the allowed apps.

How do I delete my data?

In Flowly › Settings › Delete local data. Your data is only on your iPhone. For purchase data, write to me with the Support ID at the bottom of Settings.
