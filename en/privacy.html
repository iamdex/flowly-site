Privacy

Updated November 3, 2026. Controller: Davide Espertini, hellodave@espertini.com.

On your iPhone

Sessions, history, intentions and settings stay on the device and are sent to no one. Delete them in Settings › Delete local data, or by deleting the app.

App blocking

Apps are chosen in iOS’s own list. Flowly gets only anonymous references and can’t see which apps you chose.

Purchases

Payments go through Apple. To verify purchases and subscriptions I use RevenueCat, Inc., which receives an anonymous identifier, purchase history and technical device data. Legal basis: performance of the contract. RevenueCat privacy.

Notifications

Local notifications only, scheduled on the device. No server.

Your rights

For access to or deletion of purchase data, write to hellodave@espertini.com with the Support ID at the bottom of the app’s Settings. You may also contact your data protection authority.
