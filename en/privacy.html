Privacy policy

Updated November 3, 2026

Flowly is a focus timer for iPhone made by Davide Espertini (“I”). This policy explains which data the app processes and why. In short: Flowly has no accounts, shows no ads and collects no usage analytics.

Controller and contact

Davide Espertini — hellodave@espertini.com.

Data that stays on your iPhone

Sessions, history, the intentions you write, preferences and chosen scenes are stored only on the device. They are not sent to me or to anyone else. You can delete them at any time in Settings › Delete local data, or by deleting the app.

Your intention appears on the lock screen and in widgets only if you turn that on.

App blocking (Screen Time)

If you use app blocking, you pick apps in iOS’s own system list. iOS gives Flowly only anonymous references: Flowly can’t see which apps you chose and receives no data about how you use your phone. The selection stays on the device.

Purchases (Flowly Plus)

Payments are handled by Apple through the App Store: I never receive your payment details. To verify purchases and subscriptions I use RevenueCat, Inc., which receives an anonymous identifier generated by the app, your purchase history and technical data such as device model, iOS and app version. No name, email or session data is shared. The legal basis is the performance of the purchase contract. RevenueCat’s policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

Notifications

End-of-phase alerts are local notifications scheduled on the device. Flowly uses no push notifications and has no server.

Retention and your rights

Local data stays until you delete it. Purchase data at RevenueCat is kept as long as needed to manage your purchases. You can ask for access, correction or deletion by writing to hellodave@espertini.com with the “Support ID” shown at the bottom of the app’s Settings. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Children

Flowly is not specifically directed at children and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, I will update this page and the date above.
